# Choose a paper to read

Here are papers selected by question, each with an angle to begin from. For the reading process, see [finding and reading papers](reading.md).

## A first encounter with a paper

**[ResNet](https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.03385)**  
What training problem did the authors face, what structure changed, and which comparisons supported it? Pair with [Mu Li's readings](https://github.com/mli/paper-reading), then return to [vision](vision.md).

**[Attention Is All You Need](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762)**  
Trace inputs and outputs in the architecture diagram, then ask which computation attention handles. [The illustrated explanation](https://jalammar.github.io/illustrated-transformer/) helps a first pass. Continue to [foundations and architectures](basics.md).

**[ReAct](https://react-lm.github.io/)**  
Read one complete trace: when does the model act, and what changes after feedback? Then inspect comparisons between reasoning, acting, and their combination. Continue to [agents](agent.md).

## Follow your interests

- [BERT](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.04805): how objectives shape representations and how those representations serve tasks. See [LLMs](llm.md).
- [LoRA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.09685): what reducing trainable parameters saves and how effectiveness is evaluated. See [LLMs](llm.md) and [systems](systems.md).
- [CLIP](https://github.com/openai/CLIP): image–text matching as training and how the trained model is used. See [multimodal models](multimodal.md).
- [DDPM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.11239): connections between noisy data, objectives, and generation. Pair with [generation](generation.md).
- [World Models](https://worldmodels.github.io/): combining compressed observations, dynamics prediction, and action selection. See [world models](world-model.md).
- [DreamerV3](https://danijar.com/project/dreamerv3/): learned models for behavior learning and the tasks covered by evaluation. See [RL](rl.md).
- [ACT / ALOHA](https://tonyzhaozh.github.io/aloha/): connecting demonstrations and action prediction to real robot tasks. See [embodied AI](embodied.md).
- [Diffusion Policy](https://diffusion-policy.cs.columbia.edu/): generative modeling of actions and the reason for that representation. See [generation](generation.md) and [embodied AI](embodied.md).
