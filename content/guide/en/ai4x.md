# Interdisciplinary AI: starting with questions from other fields

What interests you most may already lie outside AI: molecular properties, medical images, materials, transport, or social networks. Start with those questions, then ask where models can help.

## Begin with tasks and data

For molecular questions, explore [DeepChem](https://github.com/deepchem/deepchem) tutorials and [Therapeutics Data Commons](https://tdcommons.ai/) task definitions. Ask where inputs and labels originate and how training and test sets are split.

For medical imaging, a segmentation example in [MONAI Tutorials](https://github.com/Project-MONAI/tutorials) connects images, annotations, and evaluation.

For materials or molecular dynamics, read the background of [DeePMD-kit](https://github.com/deepmodeling/deepmd-kit), then choose your depth using disciplinary knowledge. The [AI for Science index](https://github.com/ai4s-research/awesome-ai-for-science) offers further leads.

## Graphs and time series have their own questions

For data naturally expressed as nodes and relationships, see [CS224W](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs224w/). For change over time, use [Forecasting: Principles and Practice](https://otexts.com/fpp3/) to understand trends, predictions, and evaluation. Its code uses R; for a Python project, see [tsai](https://github.com/timeseriesAI/tsai).

Establish a simple reference before judging what a complex method adds. Subject knowledge helps expose conditions easily missed in the data and task.

## Automated research is itself a question to investigate

Inspect [Sakana AI Scientist](https://github.com/SakanaAI/AI-Scientist) and [autoresearch](https://github.com/karpathy/autoresearch): what can the system modify, how does it select experiments, and how does it evaluate results? Understand each project's scope before considering expansion to other research.

For pursuing an interdisciplinary project, return to [research questions](research.md), [collaboration](contact.md), and [experiments](experiments.md).

## Find tasks by discipline

The [AI4X catalog](catalog-directions.md#topic-23) covers drug discovery, medical imaging, molecular dynamics, and more. Inspect data and evaluation before deciding which model knowledge to learn.
